At the outset, the report was brief and fairly ordinary: a rail line, a warehouse zone, a paragraph about freight. But the numbers began to vary in ways no one could specify. A young specialist from the statistics bureau was asked to compile them, compare one month with the next, and estimate the percentage of goods that never reached the market. No one could explain why; some began to speculate about a weird error in the new software. Her employer gave her wide scope and one simple aim: find the variation, trace it, terminate it — before the town's welfare budget took another hit.

一开始，这份报告简短而相当平常：一条铁路线、一个仓库区、一段关于货运的文字。但数字开始以一种谁也说不清的方式变化。统计局一位年轻的专员被派来汇编这些数据，把一个月和下一个月作比较，并估算那些从未进入市场的货物占多大比例。没人能解释原因；有人开始猜测是新软件里出了个奇怪的错误。她的雇主给了她很大的权限，目标却只有一个：找到那处异常，追出它的去向，在它再一次掏空镇上的福利预算之前把它掐断。

The first indication was a vague whisper in the ledger: shipments that seemed to vanish and then return under an alternate name. To a weary clerk this was no more than random noise, but the specialist had learned never to underestimate a small variance. She set a tentative target, allocated two assistants to the task, and began to track every entry with the emphasis of someone building a case for court. The totals seemed to indicate a simple mistake. When the aggregate came out, however, the alarm went off: this was not a partial error but a complete alteration of the record.

最早的迹象，是账簿里一句含糊的低语：有些货似乎消失了，随后又换了个名字回来。在一位疲惫的职员看来，这不过是随机的噪声；但这位专员早就学会绝不小看一处小小的偏差。她定下一个试探性的目标，拨了两名助手做这件事，然后像给法庭准备证据一样，开始逐条追踪每一笔记录。各项合计似乎表明只是个小错。然而当汇总数出来时，警报响了：这不是局部的差错，而是整份记录的彻底改动。

Each special case had a twin — no single specific entry proved anything — yet the pattern held, comparable across the whole range of accounts. One line was indicative of a larger bias: the same few names, again and again, always on the side that scored best. "Do not deny what the page shows you," her mentor said. "Testify only to what is valid." He was competent but careful, and he warned her that a temporary gap in the books could aggravate a temporal one — a delay that would look exactly like fraud.

每个特别的个案都有它的孪生兄弟——单看任何一笔具体记录都证明不了什么——可这个模式始终成立，在整片账目范围内都彼此吻合。其中一行暗示着更大的偏袒：总是那么几个名字，一次又一次，永远落在得分最高的那一边。“别否认账页给你看的东西，”她的导师说，“只为你确认有效的部分作证。”他能力很强，为人却谨慎，并提醒她：账上暂时的空缺，可能会加重时间上的空缺——那种延迟，看上去会和做假账一模一样。

The case compelled her to undergo months of dull work. She would alternate between the office and the docks, wearing out two pairs of shoes and compensating for every lost hour with an hour stolen from sleep. Her peers called her aggressive; she preferred to think of herself as brisk. "You cannot oppress a ledger," she joked, "but you can outlast it." At times fear would whirl through her and she would look for any opportunity to quit; then the charm of the puzzle came back, and she would endorse her own stubbornness.

这个案子逼着她熬过了几个月枯燥的工作。她在办公室和码头之间来回轮转，穿坏了两双鞋，每丢掉一个小时，就从睡眠里偷一个小时补回来。同行说她太好斗；她更愿意把自己看成办事爽利。“你压迫不了一本账簿，”她打趣道，“但你可以比它撑得更久。”有时恐惧会在她心里打转，她会抓住任何一个可以退出的机会；可随后谜题本身的魅力又回来了，她便再一次认可自己的固执。

The answer, when it came, was almost agreeable in its simplicity. It had been a quiet, almost gentlemanly endeavor: a clerk had been paid to agitate on behalf of a rival firm; his opponent in the union helped him alter the invoices, and the alteration spread like ink in water, beginning to diffuse through every department. The market had always been competitive, and the competition itself had been fair; the rival had simply chosen to compete by other means. He was offered an alternative — resign, and keep his pension — and he took it. For one brief season he enjoyed the advantage — then came an outraged employer, a charge of fraud, and the term of his contract was cut short. The board wanted a quiet settlement; her mentor was the first to oppose it.

答案揭晓时，简单得几乎令人愉快。这件事从头到尾干得安安静静，甚至有点体面：一名职员被收买，替一家对手公司煽风点火；他在工会里的对头帮着他改发票，而改动像水里的墨一样散开，开始扩散到每一个部门。市场一向竞争激烈，竞争本身也是公平的；那家对手不过是选择了用别的手段来争。有人给了他一个替代方案——辞职，保留养老金——他接受了。短短一季，他享受着这点优势；随后迎来的是一位暴怒的雇主、一项欺诈指控，以及被提前终止的合同期限。董事会想悄悄了结，第一个站出来反对的，是她的导师。

On the day the case closed, the town met in the old opera house — the one place where, in a democracy, anyone may complain aloud without being alienated. The mayor used the evening to highlight the underlying weakness of the system: too much trust, too little checking. "We had grown alien to our own accounts," he said. "That is the utter shame of it." A committee was formed, by careful selection, to rewrite the specification for every audit; the first draft ran to forty pages and was, in places, barely readable.

结案那天，全镇的人聚在旧歌剧院——在民主制度下，那是唯一一个可以大声抱怨而不至于被孤立的地方。镇长借这个晚上点出了制度底下的软肋：信任太多，核查太少。“我们对自己的账已经陌生了，”他说，“这才是最彻底的耻辱。”一个委员会经过仔细挑选组建起来，负责重写每一项审计的规范；初稿写了四十页，有些地方几乎没法读。

Five years later she came back — a graduate who had chosen to specialize in quiet fraud, with a specialty in audit statistics — and walked towards the same warehouse. Little had changed except the files, which had improved by gradual steps and with the aid of better software. She expected to encounter old faces; instead she found a generation that had never heard of the scandal, now a species of local myth. Yet every quarter, when the alarm sounded over some discrepancy, someone remembered her name, and the town grew a little more careful with its thrift.

五年后她回来了——一个决心专攻“安静造假”的毕业生，专业是审计统计——朝同一座仓库走去。除了档案，几乎没什么变化；档案靠着更好的软件、一步一步慢慢改进过了。她以为会遇见旧面孔，结果遇到的是一整代人：他们从没听过那桩丑闻，它如今已成了本地传说的一种。但每个季度，当某处账目对不上、警报响起时，总有人想起她的名字；而这座小镇对自己那点积蓄，也变得更谨慎了一些。
